Kauthukam - SFADC Rabee' Arts Fest '26,

Kauthukam is the biennial Rabee' Arts Fest of SFADC (Sheikh Fareed Auliya Da'wa College, Odamlaa), celebrating cultural talents and creative expressions of undergraduate students. The event is officially organized by Sheikh Fareed Auliya Da'wa College under the academic patronage of Darul Huda Islamic University to support, empower, and promote the educational and artistic development of concerned students.

About

SFADC Odamala

Sheikh Fareed Auliya Da'wa College (SFADC), Odamala is a distinguished Islamic institution located in Odamala, Malappuram, Kerala. The college is affiliated with Darul Huda Islamic University (DHIU) and is dedicated to providing integrated Islamic education, combining classical Islamic sciences with contemporary academic approaches.

Named in honor of Sheikh Fareed Auliya (R.A.), the institution strives to cultivate knowledgeable, morally responsible, and spiritually grounded scholars. It offers a comprehensive curriculum in disciplines such as Qur'anic Studies, Hadith and Related Sciences, Fiqh, Arabic Language, Da'wah, and Islamic Thought, while encouraging research, critical thinking, and community engagement.
